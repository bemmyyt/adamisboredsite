HOW TO PLAY: Click two nodes to create/remove a link (cost 15). Connected servers route traffic to the gateway. Select a node to upgrade capacity, activate an offline server, or repair damage. Every wave increases demand and may damage infrastructure. Links can relay traffic through other nodes, but each link has a throughput limit of 12. The gateway is always online.

A fictional local-only strategy game. No real networks or accounts involved.
